## OnGo: A Simplified Air Travel Booking and Management Platform
**Single-sentence project summary:** OnGo is a streamlined air travel booking and management application that enables travelers to search, book, and manage flights in one intuitive, user-focused platform.
### Project Description
Booking flights can be frustrating. People often have to jump between different websites or apps just to search for flights, book tickets, check their itinerary, or make changes. The process is cluttered with extra features that get in the way of what travelers are actually trying to do, leading to confusion, mistakes, and wasted time.
OnGo is meant to make this simpler. It brings flight search, booking, and trip management together in one easy-to-use place. Instead of overwhelming users, OnGo focuses on the tasks people care about most: finding flights, securing seats, getting reminders, and updating plans when things change. By keeping everything clear and straightforward, OnGo helps travelers plan and manage their trips with less stress and fewer headaches.
### Target Users
OnGo is designed for a broad range of travelers, with a particular focus on three groups:
*	Business travelers, who need a quick and reliable way to book trips, stay on top of their itineraries, and make changes when plans shift.
*	Families and group travelers, who need tools to coordinate multiple passengers, seat selections, and shared itineraries. Also be able easily share booking details with others in the group.
*	Frequent and occasional flyers who value a clear, straightforward experience over cluttered, feature-heavy platforms.
### Value to Users
OnGo delivers value by reducing friction throughout the travel planning process. Users benefit from having all flight-related tasks centralized in one place, which minimizes confusion and saves time. Business travelers gain peace of mind through timely reminders and straightforward flight modification options, reducing the chance of missed flights or scheduling conflicts. Families and groups benefit from simplified coordination, as OnGo makes it easier to manage multiple travelers within a single booking and share trip details with everyone involved. OnGo’s streamlined booking flow also means that spontaneous travelers can quickly book a flight to their preferred destination without unnecessary steps. Overall, OnGo improves user confidence, efficiency, and satisfaction by allowing travelers to focus on their journey rather than the booking process.
### Success Criteria
OnGo will be considered successful when:
* Most users can search and book a flight in under five minutes, without needing help or guidance.
*	At least 8 out of 10 users can manage their booking on their own — whether that’s reviewing their itinerary, selecting a seat, or making a change to their trip.
*	Users consistently rate their experience 4 out of 5 or higher when asked how easy OnGo was to use.
*	Travelers make significantly fewer booking mistakes — such as entering incorrect passenger details or choosing the wrong seat — compared to what they experience on traditional platforms.
*	More than two-thirds of users return to OnGo for their next trip within six months.
